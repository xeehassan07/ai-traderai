What Trader AI Is

Trader AI is a research and analysis platform designed to support traders with data-driven insights and structured tools.

AI-Assisted Market Research

Ask questions about markets and receive structured research responses with supporting context and identified limitations.

Chart Analysis

Upload chart screenshots and receive organised analysis of visible trends, price zones and momentum.

Strategy Testing

Describe strategies in everyday language and turn them into structured, testable trading rules.

Backtesting

Test strategy rules against historical data to evaluate potential performance under past market conditions.

Paper Trading

Practice the process of trading in a simulated environment using simulated funds.

Risk Management Tools

Define position size limits, daily loss thresholds, exposure caps and strategy pause conditions.

Market Data Providers

Trader AI uses market data from third-party providers to power its analysis and research tools.

Data Sources

Market data may include real-time, delayed, and historical data from one or more providers. The specific data sources may vary by instrument and region. Delayed data is clearly labelled where applicable.

Market data is sourced from established third-party data providers. Data may be live, delayed, or illustrative depending on the provider, instrument, and plan. All data sources and delays are clearly indicated on the platform.

Live vs Delayed Data

Some market data displayed on the platform may be delayed by 15-20 minutes depending on the exchange and data provider terms. Data labelled as "live" is intended to be as close to real-time as the provider allows, but technical latencies may apply.

AI Model Limitations

Trader AI uses artificial intelligence and machine learning models to generate analysis and observations. These models have important limitations that you should understand.

AI-generated information can be incorrect, incomplete, biased or outdated. AI models do not predict markets, guarantee outcomes, or replace human judgment. Users remain fully responsible for their own trading decisions.

No Market Prediction

AI models analyse patterns and relationships in data but cannot predict future market movements with certainty. All outputs should be treated as hypotheses, not forecasts.

Limitations of Historical Data

AI models trained on historical data may not perform well in market conditions that differ significantly from their training data. Past patterns do not necessarily repeat.

User Responsibility

You remain solely responsible for any trading decisions you make. Trader AI's tools are designed to inform your process, not to make decisions on your behalf.

Potential Biases

AI models may reflect biases present in their training data. Trader AI works to identify and mitigate such biases but cannot guarantee that outputs are free from all forms of bias.
