Market Data and AI Limitations

Market data is sourced from established third-party providers. Every data point is clearly labelled: Live, Delayed (10-15 min), Simulated, Backtested or Illustrative.

AI can be wrong. AI-generated analysis is based on visible data and patterns. It can be incorrect, incomplete, biased or outdated. It does not predict markets. Always verify findings through your own research.
