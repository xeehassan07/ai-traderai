Analyse EUR/USD market structure on the 4-hour chart
🤖

Direct Summary
EUR/USD is trading within a defined range between 1.0780 and 1.0920 on the 4-hour timeframe. The broader daily trend remains bullish, but short-term momentum is neutral.

Supporting Context
Price is above the 200-period moving average (1.0760), confirming the longer-term bullish structure. RSI is at 54, indicating neutral momentum. Support at 1.0780 has been tested three times in the past two weeks.

Alternative Scenario
If price breaks below 1.0780 with conviction, the bullish structure would be brought into question. Conversely, a break above 1.0920 could accelerate buying toward 1.1050.

Key Risks
Range-bound conditions can produce false breakouts. Key economic data releases this week could introduce volatility. AI-generated analysis may not capture all relevant factors.

Limitations
This analysis is based on visible price action and selected indicators. It does not account for fundamental factors, news events or macro conditions.

Suggested Research Step
Review the daily and weekly charts for broader context. Monitor price action around 1.0780 and 1.0920 levels. Check the economic calendar for upcoming high-impact events.
