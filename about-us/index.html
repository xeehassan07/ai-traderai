About Trader AI

We build AI-assisted research tools for independent traders — without the hype, fake promises, or hidden agendas.

Our Mission

Most trading platforms fall into one of two camps: basic charting tools that tell you nothing, or black-box systems that promise guaranteed profits. Trader AI takes a different path — we provide AI-assisted research tools that surface observations, organise data, and help you test ideas, but leave every decision in your hands.

We believe traders deserve better tools. Tools that are transparent about what they can and cannot do. Tools that label every data point — live, delayed, backtested, or illustrative. Tools that help you think, not tools that tell you what to think.

What We Build

Trader AI is an integrated platform of seven connected tools — from AI-powered chart analysis and strategy building to backtesting, paper trading, and risk management. Each tool is designed to work independently or together, depending on your workflow.

We do not execute trades, manage funds, or provide financial advice. We build research and analysis tools — nothing more, nothing less.

Transparency First

Every data point is labelled. Every limitation is stated. No hidden assumptions, no cherry-picked results.

Built for Traders

We design tools for real trading workflows — not for marketing slides. Practical, usable, and grounded in how traders actually work.

No False Promises

AI can help — but it cannot predict markets. We are clear about what our tools can and cannot do, always.

Our Approach to AI

AI is a tool, not an oracle. Our models analyse historical patterns, surface relevant information, and organise observations — but they do not predict the future. Every AI-generated output includes the context, limitations, and data sources you need to evaluate it critically.

We do not use AI to replace human judgement. We use it to support it.

Who We Serve

Trader AI is built for independent traders — whether you are a beginner learning market concepts through paper trading, an experienced trader formalising your strategy, or someone who wants to research markets with more structure. Our tools adapt to your level of experience.
