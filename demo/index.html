What the Interactive Platform Demo Covers

This interactive trading platform demo gives you a hands-on preview of the core tools and capabilities available inside Trader AI. Designed for traders at every level, it walks you through the complete workflow — from selecting a market and asking AI-powered questions, to building a custom strategy, running a backtest, and configuring risk controls. Whether you are new to algorithmic trading or an experienced trader evaluating the platform, this AI trading demo helps you understand how the platform works before you create an account.

The demo covers eight key steps that mirror the full platform experience. You begin by choosing a market — Forex, Indices, Commodities, Crypto, or Stocks — and then ask natural-language questions about market structure, trends, or technical conditions. The AI analysis preview shows how the platform processes your query and returns structured insights, including market context, technical observations, and risk disclaimers. From there, you examine chart patterns and AI-identified price zones, describe a trading idea in plain language, and watch the AI convert it into structured strategy rules.

You also get a look at the backtesting engine, which tests strategy rules against historical data and surfaces performance metrics such as net return, win rate, max drawdown, and total trades. The risk controls section demonstrates how to set exposure limits, daily loss limits, and position caps — essential tools for managing risk before entering live markets. Finally, the demo allows you to save your progress so you can pick up where you left off after registering. This interactive platform demo is designed to give you a realistic sense of the platform's capabilities, all without any financial commitment or credit card required.
