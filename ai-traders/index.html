Understanding AI Trading Strategy Types

Each strategy type is designed for different market conditions. Understanding how they work helps you choose the right approach for your trading style.

Trend Following

Capture Sustained Market Moves

Trend following AI traders use Ensemble LSTM models to identify and ride sustained directional moves across multiple timeframes. Best suited for trending forex pairs and commodities during clear policy cycles. Lower win rates but larger average gains per trade.

Model: LSTM EnsembleBest: Trending Markets
Momentum

Ride Price Acceleration

Momentum AI traders use Transformer models to detect price acceleration and enter in the direction of strength. Most effective on equity indices and volatile instruments. Higher return potential but requires strict risk controls during reversals.

Model: TransformerBest: Indices & Volatile
Mean Reversion

Exploit Price Extremes

Mean reversion AI traders use CNN-LSTM models to identify overextended prices likely to revert. Best in range-bound forex pairs with clear support and resistance. Higher win rates with smaller individual gains per trade.

Model: CNN-LSTMBest: Range-Bound Forex
Breakout

Trade Consolidation Breakouts

Breakout AI traders use GRU Ensemble models to detect consolidation and enter on confirmed breaks. Particularly effective in cryptocurrency markets. Lower win rates but winning trades can deliver outsized returns.

Model: GRU EnsembleBest: Crypto Markets
Multi-Factor

Combine Multiple Analysis Dimensions

Multi-factor AI traders combine value, momentum, quality and sentiment factors using XGBoost + LSTM hybrid models. Each factor is weighted based on historical predictive power. Commonly applied to stock selection where multiple fundamental and technical dimensions can be evaluated. Offers diversification across factors but requires more complex validation.

Model: XGBoost + LSTMBest: Stock Selection

Frequently Asked Questions

All AI trader profiles shown are illustrative simulations created to demonstrate how different strategies compare across risk and return dimensions. They are not live trading results. Every profile is clearly labelled with its data status.

Each AI trader uses a specific machine learning model (LSTM, Transformer, CNN, XGBoost etc.) trained on historical market data. The models identify patterns and generate signals based on their respective strategy types — trend following, momentum, mean reversion, breakout, or multi-factor.

No single strategy type is universally better. Performance depends on market conditions, volatility, and the specific instrument. Trend following tends to perform well in trending markets, mean reversion in range-bound conditions, and momentum during strong directional moves. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

The AI trader profiles are intended to demonstrate the platform's capabilities. You can build and test your own strategies using the AI Strategy Builder and test them with the Backtesting tool. Always conduct your own research before trading.
